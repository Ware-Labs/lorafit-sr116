### Framing hints from retained bytes
- **Observed:** `0x0017` writes and `0x0019` notifications retain prefix `FE FC` whenever ≥2 payload bytes exist after the ATT handle — consistent across both captures.
- **Observed:** Complete (non-truncated) PDUs on `0x0042`/`0x0044` are often a *single* data byte (Write_Cmd `52 42 00 XX`, Ntf `1b 44 00 XX`), while many other PDUs on those handles have `original_length` up to 256 with bodies stripped — consistent with ACK/credit bytes interleaved with bulk notifications/writes whose contents are missing from `btsnooz`.
- **Inferred (unproven UUID bind):** nRF hints map service `0x56FF` (`0x33F3`/`0x33F4`) to the `0x0042`/`0x0044` pair and `0xFF12` (`0xFF15`/`0xFF14`) to `0x0017`/`0x0019`. Confirm with GATT inspect.

### What truncation prevents
- **Unknown:** audio codec, framing, checksums, file listing commands, authentication tokens — payload bodies are largely stripped in `btsnooz`.
- **Observed:** opcode types, ATT handles, directions, timing, and *original* sizes still allow identifying *which characteristics* carried the bulk exchange.

## Missing evidence / next capture

Android `btsnooz` diagnostic truncation removes most ATT notification/write payloads. Absence of audio magic bytes in *captured* payloads does **not** mean audio was absent on-air.
